WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Golden Heyday Caterers

Golden Heyday Caterers is a catering and food-service brand situated in Kizhakkambalam, Kerala, India.

We focus on food, presentation and dining experiences for weddings, celebrations, private functions and special occasions.

Our stated service locations include Pala, Muvattupuzha and Ernakulam, Kerala.
